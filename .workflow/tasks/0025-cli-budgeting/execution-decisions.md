# Execution decisions: 0025 cli-budgeting

| ID  | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Reason                                                    | Status                           |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| D1  | Start on Linux acceptance of 0014, 0016, 0018 and 0024 (Windows reruns open).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Same rule as the other dependents.                        | ASSUMPTION for Michael to review |
| D2  | `budgets.move` uses the engine's `transferCategory` (category to category or to To Budget) and `transferAvailable` (from To Budget), the handlers behind the app's transfer and allocate menus, and verifies the written budgeted cells against the preview. Category moves keep the app's movement line in the month's budget notes; that is disclosed as a side effect.                                                                                              | One owner for allocation math and parity with the app.    | Adopted                          |
| D3  | Insufficient funds: a move that leaves the source category balance negative is refused unless `allowOverspend` is set; a move from To Budget larger than To Budget is always refused instead of the engine's silent clamp.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | The plan requires explicit insufficient-funding behavior. | ASSUMPTION for Michael to review |
| D4  | Multi-category allocation batches are not added; moves are single pairs and template application covers bulk allocation. `budgets set-amount`, carryover and holds keep their 0014 guarded paths.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Keeps each receipt atomic and verifiable.                 | ASSUMPTION for Michael to review |
| D5  | Template preview computes the templates the app would use after its own `storeNoteTemplates` refresh without writing (`effectiveTemplates`: UI-managed definitions, parsed notes for note-managed categories, cleared definitions for note-managed categories without template lines), then runs the engine's `computeTemplates` with the same force and category scope as the app's menus. Apply calls the app's own apply functions and verifies each budgeted cell. | Preview equals apply without a second template engine.    | Adopted                          |
| D6  | Template errors block preview (`INVALID_INPUT` with the engine's messages); `budgets templates` reports them read-only, like the app's "Check templates".                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Precise failure instead of a partial apply.               | Adopted                          |
| D7  | Reservations are exposed read-only through the existing `budget/get-reservations` and require the `flags.budgetReservations` preference, matching the browser hook's gate; the engine's current-month and envelope-only limits are returned as `INVALID_INPUT`.                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Preserve experimental gating.                             | ASSUMPTION for Michael to review |
| D8  | Formula templates are planned by the same engine path but not covered by a packaged fixture; browser agreement for reservations is not re-proven here (0002 owns the browser view).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Time; recorded as limitations.                            | ASSUMPTION for Michael to review |
